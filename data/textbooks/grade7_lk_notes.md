# Grade 7 Sri Lanka Curriculum Reference Notes

## Fractions & Algebra
Topic: Fractions & Algebra
Source: Grade 7 Mathematics Textbook, Chapter 4 (p. 42-48)
To add or subtract unlike fractions, convert both fractions to their Lowest Common Denominator (LCD) first, then add or subtract numerators. Always simplify the final fraction to lowest terms. For simple linear equations like `3y - 5 = 10`, undo subtraction/addition first (`3y = 15`), then divide by the coefficient (`y = 5`).

## Science - Matter & Energy
Topic: Science - Matter & Energy
Source: Grade 7 Science Textbook, Unit 3 (p. 28-35)
Matter exists primarily as solids, liquids, and gases. Solids have fixed shape and fixed volume because particles are tightly packed. Liquids have fixed volume but no fixed shape, taking the shape of their container. Gases have neither fixed shape nor fixed volume. Heat energy causes expansion and state changes (melting, boiling, evaporation).

## Sinhala Grammar
Topic: Sinhala Grammar
Source: Grade 7 Sinhala Language & Literature, Unit 2 (p. 18-24)
In Sinhala grammar, Vibhakthi (විභක්ති) show the grammatical role of nouns. Prathama Vibhakthi (ප්‍රථමා විභක්ති) marks the subject of an active sentence, while Karma Vibhakthi (කර්ම විභක්ති) marks the direct object.

## Algebra
Topic: Algebra
Source: Foundation Algebra Guide, Chapter 1 (p. 10-16)
When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents: `x^a * x^b = x^(a+b)`. Keep equations balanced by applying the same inverse operation to both sides.

## Geometry
Topic: Geometry
Source: Foundation Geometry Guide, Chapter 2 (p. 22-27)
For a circle of radius `r`, area is `pi * r^2` and circumference is `2 * pi * r`.
